Privacy

In plain English

Friends of Plymtree School (registered charity 1046679) is a small, volunteer-run charity. We only collect the information we need, and we never sell it or share it for marketing.

This website

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking.
  • There are no forms on this website itself, so it doesn’t collect any information from you.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record basic technical information (such as your IP address) to keep the service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email us

We use your name and email address only to reply to you, and we won’t pass them on to anyone else.

The 100 Club

When you join the 100 Club, we keep your name, contact details and the number of shares you hold. We use these only to run the 100 Club: recording your shares, sending your share certificate, holding the draws and contacting you if you win. The online application form is a Google Form, so Google processes the answers on our behalf.

Links to other sites

Links to Facebook, Google Forms and the Charity Commission take you to sites that have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Just email friendsofplymtreeschool@gmail.com. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.
