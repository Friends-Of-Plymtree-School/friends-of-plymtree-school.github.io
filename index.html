Plymtree C of E Primary School

Welcome to Friends of Plymtree School

We’re a charity made up of staff and parents who meet regularly and organise fundraising events, providing extra funds for our village school.

Join the 100 Club About FOPS

What we do

Making school even better for every child

1

Bring people together

We help build good relationships between staff, parents and everyone connected with the school.

2

Raise funds

Our events and activities raise money for equipment and projects, in and out of school, that enrich the children’s wider education.

3

Have fun

We organise social events for the children, their families and the wider village community to enjoy.

Your money at work

What FOPS has paid for

Thanks to everyone who comes to our events and supports our fundraising, FOPS has helped with:

  • Redeveloping the outside learning area
  • Transport for swimming galas
  • Trips to the theatre
  • Art supplies
  • IT equipment and cameras
  • Music and plays
  • Subscriptions to teaching resources
  • …and much, much more

Everyone is welcome

Our committee meets at the start of every half term, and our AGM is held every September. Come along, catch us in the playground after school, or email friendsofplymtreeschool@gmail.com.
