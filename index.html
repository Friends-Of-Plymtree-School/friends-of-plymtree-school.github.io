About us

Who we are

Friends of Plymtree School, or F.O.P.S., is a charitable organisation made up of staff and parents who meet regularly and organise fundraising events, providing additional funds for the school.

Our committee

FOPS is run by a committee of volunteers, the parents and teaching staff who give their time to plan events and look after the money we raise. We couldn’t do it without them. New faces are always welcome, however much or little time you have.

Get involved

We really value help, no matter how large or small

Come to a meeting

Our committee meetings are held at the start of every half term, and our Annual General Meeting (AGM) is in September each year. Everyone is welcome, so please don’t be shy!

Lend a hand

Don’t worry if you don’t have much spare time: every little helps. Our committee couldn’t run without the hard work of the parents and teaching staff involved.

Privacy

Your privacy, in plain English

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking, and it has no forms, so it doesn’t collect any information from you.
  • It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record basic technical information (such as your IP address) to keep the service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • If you email us, we use your name and email address only to reply to you, and we won’t pass them on to anyone else.
  • Links to Facebook and the Charity Commission take you to sites with their own privacy policies.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete any information we hold about you by emailing friendsofplymtreeschool@gmail.com. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled it, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.
