About FOPS

Friends of Plymtree School, or F.O.P.S., supporting Plymtree Church of England Primary School

Who we are

Friends of Plymtree School (FOPS) is a charitable organisation made up of staff and parents who meet regularly and organise fundraising events, providing additional funds for the school.

Our aims

Friends of Plymtree School aims to:

  • develop effective relationships between the staff, parents and others associated with the school
  • arrange events and activities that raise funds for equipment and projects in and out of school, to enhance and enrich the wider education of the pupils
  • organise social events for the enjoyment of the pupils, their families and the wider community.

What we’ve funded

  • Redeveloping the outside learning area
  • Transport for swimming galas
  • Trips to the theatre
  • Art supplies
  • IT equipment and cameras
  • Music and plays
  • Subscriptions to teaching resources
  • …and much, much more

Our committee

FOPS is run by a committee of volunteers, the parents and teaching staff who give their time to plan events and look after the money we raise. We couldn’t do it without them. The committee meets at the start of every half term, and our Annual General Meeting (AGM) is held every September.

New faces are always welcome, however much or little time you have. See Get involved or get in touch.
