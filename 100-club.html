How it works

The 100 Club is a small lottery that raises money for the school. Each share costs £15 for the year (an annual subscription, September 2026 to 2027), and you can buy more than one.

Every share is entered into each monthly draw. The first draw is on 16 October 2026 and the final draw is on August Bank Holiday Monday 2027.

Once you’ve joined, you’ll receive a share certificate.

Small lotteries licence number: SL0047.
